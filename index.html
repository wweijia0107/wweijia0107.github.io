About

I am a thesis-based M.S. student in Geography at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, working with Dr. Hongyu Zhang on ethical and responsible GeoAI, with a focus on location privacy and place-based geoprivacy.

My research connects GeoAI and Earth observation. I study the adaptation and evaluation of remote-sensing vision-language models, including their behavior under spatial distribution shift. I previously studied geospatial systems at Northern Illinois University, where I conducted research with Prof. Wei Luo.

Research interests

Responsible GeoAI & geoprivacy
Location disclosure, contextual integrity, place-based privacy, and responsible geospatial data use.
Vision-language models for Earth observation
Parameter-efficient model adaptation, model steering, and multi-label land-cover classification.
Spatial generalization & evaluation
Robustness across regions, sensors, seasons, and land-cover distributions.

M.S. thesis research · 2026–present

Privacy in Place: A Place-Category Privacy Concern Survey

Working with Dr. Hongyu Zhang, I am developing a scenario-based survey of how visited place categories, recipients of disclosed location information, and respondent characteristics influence perceived privacy concerns. The project connects contextual integrity and place-based geoprivacy with proposed ordinal mixed-effects analyses.

The protocol received an IRB exempt determination in August 2026. Survey instrument development, pilot planning, and manuscript preparation are in progress. Findings are not yet reported.

Selected projects

Remote-sensing VLM adaptation

Instruction-tuned vision-language models for multi-label land-cover classification on BigEarthNet-S2, using parameter-efficient LoRA adaptation. Focus: model steering, reproducible evaluation, and failure analysis.

Ground-penetrating radar inversion

A TransUNet framework for reconstructing 2-D subsurface permittivity maps from GPR B-scan data. Achieved hidden-test IoU of 0.873 and ranked 1st among 14 finalist teams in Kaggle GDLC-1.

Remote-sensing language-model prototype

A domain-adapted VisualGLM-6B prototype for natural-language queries over Sentinel-2 imagery and Google Earth Engine assets, combining LoRA adaptation with Gradio.

Tools: Python, R, PyTorch, LoRA/QLoRA, Git, Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, ENVI, ERDAS, and GeoDa.
